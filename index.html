Dear Chikuu

Meri jaan,

Aaj tumhara birthday hai, aur main chahta hoon ki aaj ke din tumhe sirf birthday wish nahi, balki ye bhi bataun ki tum mere liye kitni special ho.

Shayad main har baar words mein express nahi kar pata, lekin tum meri life ka woh hissa ho jiske bina sab kuch thoda adhura sa lagta hai.

Tumhari ek smile mera pura mood change kar sakti hai. Tumhari ek chhoti si baat bhi mere liye bahut important hoti hai.

Humare beech distance hai, lekin mere dil ke liye tum kabhi door nahi rahi. Faasle sirf jagah ke hain, dil ke nahi.

Main bas ye chahta hoon ki tum hamesha khush raho, smile karti raho aur apne saare dreams achieve karo.

Aur haan… zindagi kitni bhi badal jaaye, meri ek wish hamesha same rahegi— tumhara haath mere haath mein ho.

Happy Birthday, meri Chikuu. ❤️

Always yours. 🤍
