02 — A few words

For My Chikuu

Tere hone se meri duniya haseen lagti hai,
Teri har muskaan mujhe sabse khaas lagti hai.
Dua hai meri, har khushi tere kadmon mein aaye,
Aur har janam mein tu hi meri zindagi ban kar aaye.

Happy Birthday meri jaan ❤️🎂
Tum sirf meri mohabbat nahi,
meri sabse khoobsurat dua ho.

Na jaane kitni duaaon ka asar hai hamare pyaar par,
Tabhi toh har mushkil ke baad bhi hum hain ek-dusre ke saath.
Waqt badle, halaat badle, duniya chahe badal jaaye,
Bas hamara pyaar kabhi na badle,
aur tu hamesha meri rahe. ❤️

Tere birthday par bas itni si dua hai meri,
Teri har khushi mein meri khushi shamil rahe,
Aur hamari ye mohabbat aaj se bhi zyada
khoobsurat hoti rahe. 🫶🏻❤️

Chahe zindagi kitne bhi mod le,
meri ek hi khwahish rahegi—
har mod par tera haath mere haath mein ho,
aur hamara pyaar…
hamesha ke liye hum dono ke saath rahe. ❤️♾️

Faasle chahe kitne bhi ho humare darmiyaan,
mera dil hamesha tere hi paas rahega.
Har birthday par tujhe gale lagane ki khwahish rahegi,
aur ek din ye dooriyan bhi khatam hongi…

Phir tera haath pakad kar kahunga—
“Ab kahin mat jaana,
mujhe tere saath poori zindagi chalna hai.” ❤️♾️
